# Document format: team charter (`team-charter`)

A team charter records the working agreements a team makes for itself: its purpose, its values, how it communicates,
meets, decides and reviews its work, and how it handles disagreements. It is written by the team in a workshop and
revised when the team or its way of working changes. The common rules (frontmatter, naming, versioning) are in
`shared/documents/common.md`.

## Frontmatter

The common keys, plus:

| Key | Rule |
|---|---|
| `review` | the date the team plans to review the charter, `YYYY-MM-DD` |

`date` is the day of the workshop, or of the draft prepared for it.

## Headings

The headings below, in this order, translated into the document's language. A heading with nothing to say keeps a
one-line "nothing to report".

```markdown
# <Project name>: team charter

## Purpose of the team
## Values
## Working agreements
## Definition of Done and workflow
## Handling disagreements
## Open questions
## Sources
```

| Heading | What goes there |
|---|---|
| Purpose of the team | what the team exists to deliver and for whom, in a few lines |
| Values | the values the team chose, each with what it means in practice |
| Working agreements | the agreements under these subheadings: communication; meetings and working hours; decisions; reviews and quality; tools |
| Definition of Done and workflow | pointers to the project's Definition of Done and Definition of Workflow, and any team agreement on top of them |
| Handling disagreements | how the team raises and settles a disagreement, and when it escalates |
| Open questions | in a draft prepared for the workshop, the questions the team has to answer under each heading; removed once the charter is recorded |
| Sources | the documents and registers read, and the workshop notes, with their date |

## Rules

- Agreements are about the way of working; nothing in the charter evaluates or singles out a person.
- Nobody's words are attributed: no "who said what", no quotes with names, no counts of votes per person.
- Roles may appear; working hours and availability are team agreements, never personal circumstances.
- A draft (`status: draft`) carries Open questions; the recorded charter (`status: issued`) has none.
